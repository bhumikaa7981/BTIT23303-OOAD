👀 Before you dive in

Real life is basically OOP and nobody told you.

Your phone is an object. Your Instagram profile is an object. Even your dog is technically an object with attributes (fluffy, loud) and behaviors (barks, begs for food). Object-Oriented Analysis & Design is just the fancy way of teaching software to think the way you already think — in things, not steps.

In this course, you'll learn to model those "things" properly (UML diagrams, state models, use cases), then bring them to life in C++ — classes, constructors, inheritance, virtual functions, the whole glow-up.

No prior OOP experience needed. Just bring curiosity — we'll bring the semicolons.

🚗Class = BlueprintCar is a class. Your actual car in the parking lot? That's the object. One plan, infinite instances.
🧬Inheritance = Family TraitsSportsCar inherits from Car. Faster, flashier, still has four wheels and a horn.
🎭Polymorphism = Same Move, Different Vibe"speak()" means bark for a Dog, meow for a Cat. One function, many personalities.
🔒Encapsulation = Main Character PrivacyYour bank balance is private. Only your own methods get to touch it. As it should be.
